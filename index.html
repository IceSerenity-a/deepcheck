Know what you're looking at

What is a
deepfake?

A deepfake is an image, video or audio clip created or changed using AI to make a person appear to say or do something they never did.

01

Look at the details

Check face edges, lighting, hands and reflections. In videos, watch for flickering or mismatched lip movements.

02

Check the source

Find the original post and compare it with reliable sources before believing or sharing it.

03

Clues are not proof

Real media can look unusual, and convincing fakes can look natural. Verify the context too.

About

Built for the deepfake era

deepcheck. is a static educational game made for NeCCSus HackFest '26 (theme: Deepfake Era), under the project type "a simple game or quiz."

It teaches people to question media instead of trusting it. It runs fully in your browser: no account, no uploads, no server. Your best score is stored only on your own device.

Important: this site does not run an AI detector on your files. It trains human judgement, which stays useful whichever tools come and go.
